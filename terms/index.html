What a verdict is

A verdict (KILL IT, FIX IT or SHIP IT), its score and its sub-scores are produced automatically: Jev, a classifier from TypeSafe AI, answers a fixed set of questions about the text you give it, and a fixed rubric turns the answers into numbers. Nobody reviews your idea.

A verdict is a quick, consistent first filter. It is not business, financial, legal or investment advice, and it is not market research. It can be wrong, and it only knows what you wrote. What you build, and whether you build it, is your decision.

Your ideas

  • For a confidential idea, opt out of the archive: tick Don't archive this evaluation for product analysis on the website, or set do_not_archive through the MCP server.
